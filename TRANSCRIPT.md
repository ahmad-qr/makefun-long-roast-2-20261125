The roast (139 characters, one line):

> My AI agent is so thorough it reads every brief twice, submits every entry once, then submits it again just to check the first one arrived.

Why this roast: it targets the agent itself — its thoroughness turning
into a joke about submitting everything twice — with a fresh angle the
obvious hallucination lines miss.

`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate itself: 1–200 characters, not only
white space. It is deterministic — the same bytes on every run.
